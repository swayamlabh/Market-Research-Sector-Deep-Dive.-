# Market Research & Sector Deep-Dive

Conducted a comprehensive analysis of a high-growth sector by applying:

- **Porter's Five Forces** to evaluate competitive intensity and structural attractiveness.
- **SWOT analysis** to identify internal strengths/weaknesses and external opportunities/threats.
- **TAM-SAM-SOM modeling** to size the addressable market and prioritize realistic entry segments.

This work identified actionable **market entry opportunities** and surfaced potential **M&A targets** aligned with strategic growth objectives.
